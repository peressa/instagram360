# Community conduct

Instagram 360 welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, provide constructive feedback and discuss the work without personal attacks. Do not harass others, discriminate, share private information without permission or post spam.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and spaces maintained for the project. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation. Enforcement considers context and repeated behavior.

Report concerns privately through the contact options on [peressa.dev](https://peressa.dev). Do not include other people's private information in public issues. Maintenance and conduct decisions are handled by [Juan Peressa](https://github.com/peressa).
